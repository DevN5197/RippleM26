The 4 Core Pillars Explained Simply

How our four unique superpowers keep your cash safe.

Every other financial tool waits until your bank balance hits zero to warn you. Rump combines four breakthrough pillars to simulate shocks, restructure bills, and guarantee mathematical precision.

Pillar 01 · Shock & Delay Simulator

The Ripple Contagion Simulator

🧸 Explain Like I’m 5: Imagine lining up dominoes. Before you flick the first one, Rump lets you slide a "What-If" bar to see exactly which toys will fall down next week—without actually breaking anything!

Real Business Mechanics: You drag interactive delay sliders (e.g., customer pays +12 days late) and Rump instantly recalculates 30 to 180 days of your cash flow, pointing out future shortfall breach dates with crisp red alert nodes.

Pillar 02 · Bite-Sized Deliveries

Auto-Phase Purchase Order Tranches

🧸 Explain Like I’m 5: Trying to swallow a giant pizza in one bite makes you choke! Instead, Rump cuts the giant pizza into 3 small slices so you only pay for one slice at a time as you eat it.

Real Business Mechanics: When a bulky upfront inventory Purchase Order would crash your safety threshold, Auto-Phase restructures the order into 3 staggered delivery tranches (e.g. Day 1, Day 12, Day 24) aligned with your incoming customer cash.

Pillar 03 · Direct Supplier Lock

Tri-Party Split-Escrow

🧸 Explain Like I’m 5: Imagine you owe your supplier money, but your customer hasn’t paid you yet. Split-Escrow is like having a helpful referee: the moment your customer pays, the referee automatically hands your supplier their exact share so shipments never stop!

Real Business Mechanics: Split-Escrow establishes a legally binding tri-party payment pipe linking your delayed customer receivable directly to your upstream supplier payable, routing committed settlement fractions instantly.

Pillar 04 · Mathematical Perfection

Exact Integer-Paise & Chaos Guardrails

🧸 Explain Like I’m 5: Instead of guessing how many coins are in your jar, Rump counts every single coin down to the tiniest 1 Paisa—and puts a locked ceiling and floor on your jar so nobody can ever type a fake number that breaks your math.

Real Business Mechanics: Standard computers make floating-point rounding drifts over 30 days. Rump stores every rupee strictly as whole Integer Paise (`1 ₹ = 100 Paise`) with strict server-side validation bounds preventing upper-bound corruption.
